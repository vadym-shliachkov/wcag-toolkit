# 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AAA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Interaction triggers motion animation; AAA.

## What is required

Provide a way to disable motion, unless it is essential to the functionality or the information.

## How to test

1. Turn on the system reduce-motion setting and the page's own settings.
2. Trigger transitions, parallax and zooming.

**Expected result:** Provide a way to disable motion, unless it is essential to the functionality or the information. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** Scrolling triggers non-essential motion that cannot be disabled.

## How to fix

Respect prefers-reduced-motion or provide a reliable toggle; replace motion with static feedback.

## Example

**Before:** Expanding an entry makes a panel fly in dramatically.

**After:** With reduced motion, the panel appears without moving.

## Exceptions and false positives

Essential motion is allowed. A change of color or opacity without movement does not automatically fall under the definition of motion animation.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 2.3.3, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Turn on the system reduce-motion setting and the page's own settings; trigger transitions, parallax and zooming.
