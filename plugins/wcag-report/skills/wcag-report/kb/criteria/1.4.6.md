# 1.4.6 Contrast (Enhanced)

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AAA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Text and images of text; AAA.

## What is required

Contrast of at least 7:1; 4.5:1 for large text.

## How to test

1. Measure the final colors and check all states.
2. Classify the font size and weight.

**Expected result:** Contrast of at least 7:1; 4.5:1 for large text. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** In an AAA profile, regular text has a contrast of 5:1.

## How to fix

Choose colors that reach 7:1 for regular text.

## Example

**Before:** A form hint has a contrast of 5.4:1.

**After:** A color with 8.2:1 is chosen.

## Exceptions and false positives

The same main exceptions apply for inactive, incidental and invisible text and for logotypes; do not apply the AAA thresholds as mandatory for AA.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. For this criterion it is especially important to compare the programmatic data with the actual presentation.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.4.6, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Measure the final colors and check all states; classify the font size and weight.
