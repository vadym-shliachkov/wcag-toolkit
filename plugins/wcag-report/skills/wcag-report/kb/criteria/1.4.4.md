# 1.4.4 Resize Text

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Text, except captions and images of text.

## What is required

Enlarging text up to 200% without assistive technology does not lose content or functionality.

## How to test

1. Enlarge the text or zoom to 200%.
2. Go through forms, menus and messages.
3. Check for clipping and whether elements remain available.

**Expected result:** Enlarging text up to 200% without assistive technology does not lose content or functionality. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** At 200%, a button's text is clipped and its function cannot be determined or carried out.

## How to fix

Remove fixed heights; allow wrapping and scaling.

## Example

**Before:** A two-line label disappears in a fixed-height card.

**After:** The card grows with its content.

## Exceptions and false positives

Horizontal scrolling is not always a failure of this criterion; its conditions are checked separately under 1.4.10.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.4.4, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Enlarge the text or zoom to 200%; go through forms, menus and messages; check for clipping and whether elements remain available.
