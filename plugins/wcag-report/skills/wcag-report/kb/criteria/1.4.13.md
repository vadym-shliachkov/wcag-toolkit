# 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Additional content appears and disappears on pointer hover or keyboard focus.

## What is required

Make the content dismissible without moving focus or the pointer, make it possible to move the pointer over it, and keep it visible until the trigger is removed, the user dismisses it, or its information is no longer valid.

## How to test

1. Open the additional content with the mouse and with the keyboard.
2. Move the pointer onto the popup.
3. Press Escape.
4. Wait and check that it persists.

**Expected result:** Make the content dismissible without moving focus or the pointer, make it possible to move the pointer over it, and keep it visible until the trigger is removed, the user dismisses it, or its information is no longer valid. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** A tooltip disappears when the user tries to move the pointer onto it, or it cannot be dismissed while it covers text.

## How to fix

Add dismissal, a stable hover area and persistent display.

## Example

**Before:** A term's explanation vanishes as the pointer moves toward it.

**After:** The pointer moves freely between the term and the tooltip.

## Exceptions and false positives

A dismissal mechanism is not required if the additional content communicates an input error or does not obscure or replace other content. Criterion exception: the visual presentation is controlled by the user agent and is not modified by the author.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.4.13, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Open the additional content with the mouse and with the keyboard; move the pointer onto the popup; press Escape; wait and check that it persists.
