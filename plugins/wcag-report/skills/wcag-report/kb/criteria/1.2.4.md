# 1.2.4 Captions (Live)

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Live audio in synchronized media.

## What is required

Provide captions for live audio content.

## How to test

1. Open the live stream.
2. Check access to the captions.
3. Compare speech, delay and meaningful audio information.

**Expected result:** Provide captions for live audio content. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** A meeting broadcast contains an audio discussion without captions.

## How to fix

Arrange synchronized captioning and a control for displaying it.

## Example

**Before:** During a meeting broadcast, questions are only heard as voice.

**After:** Questions and answers are shown as captions.

## Exceptions and false positives

Do not automatically extend the requirement to every audio-only broadcast: this criterion concerns synchronized media. Permanently visible open captions are acceptable; a toggle is needed for the chosen implementation of closed captions, not as a separate universal requirement. The Understanding document distinguishes broadcasting from two-way conversation: do not automatically infer that an application must caption every private call. Establish the nature of the event and the role of the content provider.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.2.4, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Open the live stream; check access to the captions; compare speech, delay and meaningful audio information.

## Code patterns

The scenario is given above. Code alone is not enough to test media, processes and semantics.
