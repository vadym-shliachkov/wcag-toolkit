# 2.4.13 Focus Appearance

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AAA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

A visible keyboard focus indicator; AAA.

## What is required

The indicator area is at least as large as the area of a 2 CSS px thick perimeter of the unfocused component or sub-component; the same pixels have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 between the focused and unfocused states.

## How to test

1. Capture images without focus and with focus.
2. Measure the area and the contrast change.
3. Check the exceptions.

**Expected result:** The indicator area is at least as large as the area of a 2 CSS px thick perimeter of the unfocused component or sub-component; the same pixels have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 between the focused and unfocused states. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** An author-supplied indicator has insufficient area or contrast change.

## How to fix

Make an indicator with sufficient area and contrast change.

## Example

**Before:** Focus is shown only by a small dot.

**After:** A 3px outline with a verified contrast change surrounds the button.

## Exceptions and false positives

The exceptions concern a user agent indicator that the author cannot change, or the author not having modified the indicator and its background; check the exact conditions. This is not a rule that "any 2px border always passes".

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. For this criterion it is especially important to compare the programmatic data with the actual presentation.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 2.4.13, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Capture images without focus and with focus; measure the area and the contrast change; check the exceptions.
