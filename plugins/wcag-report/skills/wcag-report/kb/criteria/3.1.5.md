# 3.1.5 Reading Level

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AAA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

After removal of proper names and titles, the text requires reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level; AAA.

## What is required

Provide supplemental content or a version that does not require a higher reading level.

## How to test

1. Assess the complexity with language and subject-matter expertise.
2. Check the simplified version and its completeness.

**Expected result:** Provide supplemental content or a version that does not require a higher reading level. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** Complex conditions are available only as difficult text with no supplemental material.

## How to fix

Prepare a plain-language version, explanations and visual support.

## Example

**Before:** The participation rules consist of complex multi-step wording.

**After:** A simple step-by-step version is available.

## Exceptions and false positives

A numeric readability score is a signal, not a final conclusion; age, language and context matter.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 3.1.5, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Assess the complexity with language and subject-matter expertise; check the simplified version and its completeness.

The scenario is given above. Code alone is not enough to test media, processes and semantics.
