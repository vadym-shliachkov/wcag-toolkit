# 2.4.12 Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced)

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AAA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

A component receives keyboard focus; AAA.

## What is required

No part of the component is hidden by author-created content.

## How to test

1. Repeat the pass with sticky and pop-up regions.
2. Check the component's whole outline.

**Expected result:** No part of the component is hidden by author-created content. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** The lower part of a focused button is covered by a sticky bar.

## How to fix

Ensure the component is fully visible when focused.

## Example

**Before:** A banner covers a quarter of a button.

**After:** The button scrolls fully into a clear area.

## Exceptions and false positives

Do not apply AAA strictness to an AA report. Take into account the normative notes on user-movable interface and on content opened by the user.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. For this criterion it is especially important to compare the programmatic data with the actual presentation.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 2.4.12, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Repeat the pass with sticky and pop-up regions; check the component's whole outline.
