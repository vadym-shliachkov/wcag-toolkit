# 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Text and images of text, including labels, placeholder text and states.

## What is required

Contrast of at least 4.5:1; 3:1 for large text.

## How to test

1. Get the final colors, taking transparency and the background into account.
2. Measure without rounding to the threshold.
3. Check all states and text over images.

**Expected result:** Contrast of at least 4.5:1; 3:1 for large text. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** A regular-size label has a measured ratio of 3.2:1.

## How to fix

Change the colors or the background to reach the threshold; check the contrast again in each state.

## Example

**Before:** A schedule label almost blends into the background.

**After:** The color has been replaced and the measurement gives 6.1:1.

## Exceptions and false positives

Large text is at least 18 pt, or 14 pt bold, roughly 24 and 18.67 CSS px. There are exceptions for inactive, incidental and invisible text and for logotypes.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. For this criterion it is especially important to compare the programmatic data with the actual presentation.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.4.3, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Get the final colors, taking transparency and the background into account; measure without rounding to the threshold; check all states and text over images.
