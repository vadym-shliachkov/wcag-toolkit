# 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum)

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

A component receives keyboard focus.

## What is required

Author-created content does not entirely hide the component.

## How to test

1. Move through the elements under sticky bars, banners and open regions.
2. Record whether the component is visible when it receives focus.

**Expected result:** Author-created content does not entirely hide the component. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** When focused, a button is entirely hidden by a sticky banner.

## How to fix

Fix the scrolling, scroll-padding or the position of the overlapping layer.

## Example

**Before:** A field slides completely under a fixed header.

**After:** Scrolling keeps the field visible.

## Exceptions and false positives

Partial obscuring is not a failure of 2.4.11 specifically; the AAA criterion 2.4.12 covers it. For user-movable interface and content opened by the user, take the normative notes into account.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. For this criterion it is especially important to compare the programmatic data with the actual presentation.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 2.4.11, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Move through the elements under sticky bars, banners and open regions; record whether the component is visible when it receives focus.
