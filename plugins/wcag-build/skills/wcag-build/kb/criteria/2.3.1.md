# 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold

WCAG 2.2 · Level **A**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Flashes in any content.

## What is required

No more than three flashes in any 1-second period, or the flashes are below the general flash and red flash thresholds.

## How to test

1. Collect the dynamic states.
2. Examine video and animation with a suitable analysis.
3. Take area, luminance and the red threshold into account.

**Expected result:** No more than three flashes in any 1-second period, or the flashes are below the general flash and red flash thresholds. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** The material exceeds the frequency threshold and the corresponding area-and-luminance threshold.

## How to fix

Remove the flashes or reduce their characteristics to within the permitted thresholds.

## Example

**Before:** An alarm signal quickly fills a large area with bright flashes.

**After:** A static warning is used.

## Exceptions and false positives

A counter of CSS animations alone cannot prove that a threshold is exceeded. Manually playing potentially dangerous material is not a required test method.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 2.3.1, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Collect the dynamic states; examine video and animation with a suitable analysis; take area, luminance and the red threshold into account.

## Code patterns

The scenario is given above. Code alone is not enough to test media, processes and semantics.
