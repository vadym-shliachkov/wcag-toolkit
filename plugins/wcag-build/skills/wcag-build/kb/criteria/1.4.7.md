# 1.4.7 Low or No Background Audio

WCAG 2.2 · Level **AAA**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Prerecorded audio-only content that is primarily speech, is not a CAPTCHA and is not an audio logo; AAA.

## What is required

There is no background sound, it can be turned off, or it is at least 20 dB quieter than the speech.

## How to test

1. Listen to the speech.
2. Check for a separate way to turn off the background, or measure the relative levels.

**Expected result:** There is no background sound, it can be turned off, or it is at least 20 dB quieter than the speech. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** Background music masks the speech and cannot be turned off, and the difference in levels is insufficient.

## How to fix

Remove the background, provide a separate control, or lower its level.

## Example

**Before:** A narration about a route plays over loud music.

**After:** A clean speech track is available.

## Exceptions and false positives

Singing and rapping are excluded; occasional background sounds lasting 1–2 seconds are allowed. Judging by ear does not replace measuring the 20 dB.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.4.7, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Listen to the speech; check for a separate way to turn off the background, or measure the relative levels.

## Code patterns

The scenario is given above. Code alone is not enough to test media, processes and semantics.
