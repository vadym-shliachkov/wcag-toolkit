# 1.1.1 Non-text Content

WCAG 2.2 · Level **A**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Images, icons, charts, diagrams, CAPTCHA, graphical controls.

## What is required

Provide text that serves the same purpose; exclude decorative content from what assistive technologies present.

## How to test

1. Determine the purpose in context.
2. Read the accessible name and description.
3. Compare them with the information in the image.
4. Separately check image links and background images.

**Expected result:** Provide text that serves the same purpose; exclude decorative content from what assistive technologies present. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** A diagram conveys a route that is not in the text; an icon button has no name.

## How to fix

Provide a short alternative; move complex data into an accessible table or description; give decorative img elements alt="".

## Example

**Before:** An irrigation diagram shows three zones without a description.

**After:** The zones and watering intervals are listed next to it; the alt points to this description.

## Exceptions and false positives

Decorative, invisible and formatting content may be ignored; media, tests, sensory experiences and CAPTCHA have special conditions. The word "image" in alt does not by itself prove a failure. For CAPTCHA, describe the purpose and provide alternatives for different types of sensory perception; for media, for a test that would lose its meaning in text, and for content that is primarily a sensory experience, provide at least descriptive identification.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. For this criterion it is especially important to compare the programmatic data with the actual presentation.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 1.1.1, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Determine the purpose in context; read the accessible name and description; compare them with the information in the image; separately check image links and background images.
