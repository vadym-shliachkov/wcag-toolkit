# 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable

WCAG 2.2 · Level **A**

A practical summary in our own words; the exact obligations are set by the normative text and its definitions.

## Applicability

Content sets a time limit for an action, for reading or for a session.

## What is required

Allow the user to turn the limit off, to adjust it in advance to at least 10 times the default, or to extend it after a warning, with at least 20 seconds for a simple action and at least 10 extensions.

## How to test

1. Find the timers.
2. Wait for the warning.
3. Measure the time allowed to respond.
4. Check the settings and repeated extensions.

**Expected result:** Allow the user to turn the limit off, to adjust it in advance to at least 10 times the default, or to extend it after a warning, with at least 20 seconds for a simple action and at least 10 extensions. All applicable states and normative exceptions have been checked.

**Fails when:** A form is cleared after one minute with no way to extend the time.

## How to fix

Provide adjustment or a warning with a working extension; preserve the data entered.

## Example

**Before:** Seat selection resets after 60 seconds.

**After:** A warning gives 30 seconds and allows at least 10 extensions.

## Exceptions and false positives

Exceptions: a real-time event, an essential time limit, or a limit longer than 20 hours; essentiality must be justified, not merely claimed.

## Automation and evidence

A tool can collect candidates and measurements; the verdict depends on applicability, content, exceptions and observed behavior. The absence of an automated signal is not a pass. The main conclusion requires a person to carry out the scenario, or an observed interactive run; a static DOM alone is not enough.

- URL/route, interface state, date, sign-in method and the steps tested.
- Viewport in CSS px, zoom level, input method, user agent, and assistive technologies if used.
- The specific element/media segment/process step, reproducible observations and the actual result.
- The normative condition of 2.2.1, the exceptions checked, and an explanation of the barrier to the user.
- For this test, record: Find the timers; wait for the warning; measure the time allowed to respond; check the settings and repeated extensions.

## Code patterns

The scenario is given above. Code alone is not enough to test media, processes and semantics.
